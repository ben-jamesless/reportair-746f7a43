Built for the build

Client-ready event build reports in 10 minutes.

BuildFolder is the daily reporting tool for event-build crews — activations, exhibitions, conferences, festivals, golf tournaments, film sets, venues, and corporate events. Capture and sort site photos, then export a polished PDF or a client-safe link in minutes. No more rebuilding decks on the train home.

Who it's for

One tool. Every build. BuildFolder is used by production managers, agency owners, and venue ops teams across these event types:

Music festivals

Build phase reporting across stages, infrastructure, and vendor setups. Track 20+ areas simultaneously — main stage, arena stage, vendor village, power infrastructure. Daily reports sent to the production manager before site closes; photo evidence for H&S sign-off and client handover.

Golf tournaments

Hole-by-hole setup documentation for events at PGA Tour, LPGA, and DP World Tour scale. Sponsor activation tracking with photo proof, daily course-walk reports to the tournament director, and flagging of incomplete branding installs before client walkthrough.

Film sets

Shooting locations, set builds, and location dressing. Continuity photo logs shared between shoots and wrap, daily updates to the location manager, and insurance-ready evidence of site condition before and after.

Brand activations

Pop-ups, roadshows, and retail installations. Capture build quality and brand compliance at every activation, flag missing assets or off-brand execution instantly, and give clients a live link to the day's install progress.

Venues

Fit-outs, installs, and turnarounds across permanent and temporary venues. Document setup zone by zone — staging, rigging, AV, dressing, front-of-house — with photo evidence for client walk-throughs and venue handover.

Corporate events

AV, staging, branding installs for conferences, awards ceremonies, and product launches. Client-ready branded report generated on the day of setup, with before/after documentation for venue handover.

How it works — three steps

Build to report. No rebuilds. No chasing photos. Just a clean report, ready to share.

  1. Capture. Shoot on site, tag as you go. Take photos as the build happens and upload them directly from the field — any device, any location. Flag issues instantly.
  2. Upload & sort. Photos auto-organise by area, day, and status. Add notes, set zone status (On track, Needs discussion, Concern, Complete), and the structured report builds itself.
  3. Export & share. One click for a branded PDF, or a shareable client link that opens in any browser — read-only, password-protectable, no client account required.

FAQ — the honest answers

Who is this actually for?

Production managers, agency owners, and venue ops teams running live events, exhibitions, and brand activations. If your day ends with you rebuilding a deck on the train home — you're our person.

Can I brand the PDF with our logo and colours?

Yes. Drop in your logo and your brand colour and every export comes out with your cover page, header, and footer. White-label PDFs (no BuildFolder watermark) are included on the Studio tier.

What does the PDF look like?

Cover with your client's name and event title, then photos grouped by zone and time, each zone tagged with a clear status — On track, Needs discussion, Concern, or Complete. Print-clean. Client-safe. A sample lands in the welcome email.

How is this different from a shared Google Drive?

Drive is a bucket. BuildFolder is a workflow — auto-tagging, structured reports, branded export, audit history. The deck-build step disappears.

What does it cost?

Solo HK$128/mo, Crew HK$298/mo, Studio HK$688/mo. Annual billing saves 20%. 14-day free trial on every tier.

Can multiple people work on the same event at once?

Yes. Invite your team to any event and everyone works in the same project in real time. A site photographer uploads from the ground, a site manager adds notes from the office, and the producer generates the report — all without stepping on each other.

Can I share a report with a client who doesn't have an account?

Yes. Generate a shareable link for any daily report which your client can open in any browser. The link shows them a clean, read-only view of that day and the overall project's progress.
